Our Story

Where Art Meets Flavor

The Story

Frida & Diego

Named after the legendary Mexican artistic couple, Frida Kahlo and Diego Rivera, our restaurant captures the passionate, colorful spirit of Mexican culture. Every corner tells a story of art, love, and tradition.

The Atmosphere

Vibrant murals, warm lighting, and unmistakable Mexican aesthetic. Casual yet charming—perfect for starting your evening in style.

Rainbow District

In the heart of Schöneberg's famous Regenbogenkiez, surrounded by Berlin's most vibrant nightlife.
