Mexican Food in Berlin-Schöneberg

Frida & Diego is a Mexican restaurant and cocktail bar in Berlin-Schöneberg, near Wittenbergplatz and Nollendorfplatz. Join us at Fuggerstraße 28 for tacos, fajitas and enchiladas. We open daily at 17:00.

Fajitas, tacos and enchiladas

Fajitas come with wheat tortillas, guacamole, herb sour cream and salsa mexicana. Our tacos and enchiladas are served with salad, rice and mashed beans.

Vegetable options

Choose vegetable fajitas, vegetable quesadillas or vegetable tacos. Check the menu for ingredients and ask our team about allergens.

Getting here

Frida & Diego is in Schöneberg near the Wittenbergplatz and Nollendorfplatz U-Bahn stations. Our regular opening hours are daily from 17:00 to midnight.
