Margaritas & Cocktails in Berlin-Schöneberg

At our cocktail bar on Fuggerstraße, you can order a classic Margarita, a frozen version or an alcohol-free alternative.

Classic Margarita

Tequila, Triple Sec and citrus. A classic choice if you like a bright citrus drink.

Frozen Margarita and Paloma

The Frozen Margarita is served blended with ice. The Paloma pairs tequila with grapefruit soda and citrus.

Alcohol-free option

The Virgin Margarita combines agave, pineapple, orange, citrus and salt. See our cocktail menu for ingredients and current prices.
